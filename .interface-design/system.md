# hibll.de interface

## Direction
Calm waiting-room / forest clearing. Warm cream paper, moss green, one terracotta SOS. German, smartphone-first.

## Who
A person in a hard moment, often on a phone. They need the next concrete step, not a catalog.

## Signature
Every screen answers: „Was kannst du jetzt tun?“ Left-edge path cards + persistent checklist. SOS visible, not clickbait.

## Depth
Subtle warm shadows + quiet forest borders. No rainbow category colors.

## Spacing
8px base. Touch targets ≥ 44px.

## Tokens
`--forest-*`, `--cream-*`, `--amber`, `--sos`. No raw slate/blue leftovers on product pages.

## Content rule
No invented numbers, hours, or operator data. Unverified entries show „Stand prüfen“.
